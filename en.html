About Mayara

Image is not about clothes.
It's about coherence.

I grew up surrounded by women with a refined eye, where quality and elegance mattered more than trends. I worked in fashion, lived behind the scenes of television in Rio de Janeiro and, at 22, started from zero in England — eventually reaching the corporate world of one of the largest law firms in the world.

There I saw something I couldn't ignore: accomplished women, in positions of leadership, who didn't feel represented by their own image. I recognised myself in that. So I trained in image consultancy and colour analysis — first to transform myself.

"When your image is aligned with who you are, everything around you starts to respond differently."
